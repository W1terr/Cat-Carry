# Cat Carry

Crimson Desert style cat carrying for Skyrim: hold Activate on a house cat to kneel, pick it up and carry it in your
arms, hold Activate again to put it down. An SKSE plugin, no ESP.

- You turn to the cat, step closer, kneel and lift it with both hands (procedural arm IK, fingers, spine lean, head
  look). The cat is held upright against your chest, a bit smaller than on the floor so every cat fits your arms.
- The cat comes alive in your arms: legs and tail hang and sway, it breathes, looks around, kneads with its front paws;
  now and then you pet it.
- Piglet (optional add-on) has his own little show: a curly tail wiggle, ear twitches, a sniff around, and after you
  pet him he nuzzles you and kicks his legs for joy.
- Walk around with the cat; a tap on Activate still opens doors. If you're attacked, the cat is put down at once.
- Every setting can be tuned live in SKSE Menu Framework (or in CatCarry.ini), in all the languages Skyrim ships with
  (English, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Polish, Russian, Japanese, Chinese).
- More pets can be added with a small animal file (SKSE\Plugins\CatCarry\Animals\*.ini: race, size, head pose).

## Requirements

- SKSE, Address Library for SKSE Plugins
- House Cats- Mihail Monsters and Animals
- Optional: SKSE Menu Framework (settings menu), Piglet - The courageous piggy companion (add-on in the installer)
